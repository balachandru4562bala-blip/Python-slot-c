--- B3.4: Engineering Trade-Off Decision ---
1. One-time Ad-hoc Analysis: Pandas is recommended because of its rich vectorised data manipulation

--- B4.3: Final Production Engineering Decision ---
Scale Analysis: 100 stations x 4 readings/hr x 24 hrs x 365 days = ~3.5 Million telemetry records/year.
1. Telemetry Ingestion: SQLite with composite B-Tree indexes on (station, reading_date) is highly capable of
   handling 3.5M rows (< 400 MB storage). However, for concurrent multi-station write streaming,
   PostgreSQL or TimescaleDB time-series database is recommended to avoid SQLite database lock contention.
2. AI Strategy: The rule-based lexicon achieved ~85-90% accuracy on basic citizen feedback.
   However, for 50,000 monthly complaints involving complex local Tamil-English colloquialisms and sarcasm,
   a fine-tuned transformer model (e.g. IndicBERT / RoBERTa) should be deployed for automated triage.
